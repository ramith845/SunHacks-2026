Walk-around tracking · free

  1. Install FREED Tracker (free) on the iPhone.
  2. Receiver IP , port . Tap Start.
  3. Press M where the shot starts and walk. Free version sends for 3 min per Start: just tap Start again.

Then press C to calibrate (5 quick moves). Only change these if calibration says the step looked vertical.

Walk-around tracking · ZIG SIM Pro ($3.99)

  1. Install ZIG SIM Pro on the iPhone.
  2. Settings: Protocol UDP, Format JSON, IP , Port , rate 60.
  3. Sensor: ARKit, mode DEVICE. Start.
  4. Stand where the shot begins, press M (Set mark).

Rotation wrong?

Press C or ⟲ Calibrate, then follow the moves on screen: tilt up, pan right, one step forward. It learns how your phone's axes map to the camera, and is remembered per tracking app.
